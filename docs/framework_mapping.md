# Mapping to reporting frameworks

How each figure the tracker produces maps to the disclosures it would feed.

| Tracker output | SEBI BRSR (Principle 6, Essential Indicators) | GRI Standards |
|---|---|---|
| Energy consumed, GJ, and energy from renewable sources | Total energy consumption and energy intensity, split by renewable and non renewable sources | GRI 302-1 Energy consumption within the organization, 302-3 Energy intensity |
| Water consumed, kl, and water intensity | Water withdrawal and consumption, water intensity | GRI 303-3 Water withdrawal, 303-5 Water consumption |
| Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions, t CO2e, and intensity | Total Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions and their intensity | GRI 305-1 Direct (Scope 1), 305-2 Energy indirect (Scope 2), 305-4 GHG emissions intensity |
| Scope 3 emissions (freight, business travel) | Scope 3 emissions (BRSR Leadership Indicators) | GRI 305-3 Other indirect (Scope 3) |
| Waste generated and waste recycled, diversion rate | Waste generated and waste recovered through recycling | GRI 306-3 Waste generated, 306-4 Waste diverted from disposal |

Notes
* Scope 2 is reported location based with the CEA grid factor; a market based figure would need supplier specific or
  green tariff data.
* Refrigerant leakage is estimated from top up quantities (the mass balance approach), the usual method when
  leak tests are not metered.
